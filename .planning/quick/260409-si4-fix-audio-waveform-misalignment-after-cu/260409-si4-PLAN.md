<objective>
Fix audio waveform misalignment after cut (split) and move (reorder) operations in the editor.

Root cause: `reorderScenes` in editor-store.ts only updated `audio.from` (timeline position) when remapping audio items to new scene positions. It did NOT update `durationInFrames` (which may extend beyond the owning scene) or `startFrom` (the source audio offset for waveform display). After a split+move, audio blocks end up at wrong positions with wrong durations and wrong waveform slices.

Fix: Rewrite the audio remapping in `reorderScenes` to properly decompose each audio item into per-scene segments, then relocate each segment to match its owning scene's new position with correct `from`, `durationInFrames`, and `startFrom`.
</objective>
