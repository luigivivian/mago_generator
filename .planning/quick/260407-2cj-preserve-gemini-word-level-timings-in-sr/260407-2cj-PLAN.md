<objective>
Stop destroying Gemini's real word-level subtitle timestamps in the reels pipeline.

Today `align_srt_with_script` (transcriber.py:227-328) takes Gemini's 4-5 word chunks with tight, real timestamps and rebuckets them into N equal time slices per cena, replacing chunked text with `cena.narracao`. This makes subtitles drift off the audio and gives `video_builder.concat_clips_with_audio` fake bucket durations to trim against, so the final reel paces wrong.

Rewrite alignment to keep Gemini's chunks as the authoritative SRT (text and timing untouched), and instead text-match each cena.narracao to a contiguous span of Gemini chunks using `difflib.SequenceMatcher`. Emit `scene_timings` with real per-cena spans `{index, start, end, duration, narracao}`. Also save `subtitles_raw.srt` as backup, and fix the latent `step_state.tts` vs `step_state.srt` key mismatch the editor uses to read `scene_timings`.

Purpose: Subtitles sync to audio, scene durations reflect real narration, video pacing improves, raw Gemini output is recoverable, and the editor actually reads the timings the backend already writes.
Output: Rewritten alignment function + pytest contract, raw SRT backup on disk, frontend reads from `stepState.srt.scene_timings` (not `stepState.tts`), single atomic commit touching all four files.
</objective>
