## Decisions Made
- SubtitleOverlay uses useCurrentFrame() directly for frame-based visibility -- no fps conversion needed since EditorSubtitle stores frame numbers
- Img imported from "remotion" core, Video and Audio from "@remotion/media" -- different export packages in Remotion 4.x
- Transitions only inserted between adjacent scenes, skipped when type is "none" or durationFrames is 0
- calculateMetadata computes total duration as sum of scene durations minus sum of transition durations, floored at 1 frame
