## Decisions Made
- PointerSensor with 5px distance constraint to distinguish click (select) from drag (reorder)
- Trim handle tracking uses document-level listeners for smooth tracking even when pointer leaves block bounds
- MIN_DURATION_FRAMES = 15 (0.5s) as floor to prevent zero-length scenes
- Ruler tick interval adapts to zoom: 10s intervals at low zoom, 1s at high zoom
